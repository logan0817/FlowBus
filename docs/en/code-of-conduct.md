# Code of conduct

[中文](../../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

FlowBus aims for direct, respectful collaboration based on evidence. This applies to issues, PRs, reviews, documentation discussions, and other project communication.

Be patient with different experience levels. Focus on reproducible facts, explain review feedback, offer actionable suggestions, correct mistakes, and respect contributors' time.

Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information or unauthorized contact details, repeated unrelated promotion, and deliberate public disclosure of exploitable vulnerabilities are unacceptable. Maintainers are not obliged to support unrelated application work.

Maintainers may warn participants, edit or hide content, close discussions, or restrict participation for severe or repeated violations. Report conduct concerns privately to `notwalnut@163.com`; do not expose private details in public issues. Follow the [security policy](security.md) for vulnerabilities.
